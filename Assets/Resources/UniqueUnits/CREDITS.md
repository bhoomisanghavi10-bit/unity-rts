# Unique Units — third-party rig/animation credits

## War Elephant

The armored elephant mesh/textures were generated by the project owner via
Meshy AI (project-owned asset, not third-party). Its **skeleton and all 4
animation clips (Idle/Walk/Attack/Die)** come from a separate, real,
third-party rigged model:

**CC Attribution (CC-BY)** — commercial use is allowed, but the author must
be credited.

| Asset used | Author | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Rig + Idle1/walk/Attack1/Die animation clips ("Asian elephant") | planeta-elefante | https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/asian-elephant-b84c5473df474f30b9637404ae42be61 |

License terms: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Only the skeleton and animation data were used — the source model's own mesh
was discarded, and the Meshy-generated armored elephant mesh was bound onto
this skeleton instead (Blender, `ARMATURE_AUTO` weights). See
`docs/SESSION_LOG.md` for the rigging session's full methodology, including
two real export-artifact fixes required to make the source clips usable
(a spurious object-level scale=0.01 channel, and spurious per-bone location
noise — both stripped, keeping only bone rotation curves).

An "African Bush Elephant" model (also planeta-elefante, also CC-BY) was
initially downloaded and evaluated as a possible Death-animation source, but
was **not used** in the final asset — flagging here only so a future session
doesn't wonder why it's sitting in Downloads.
